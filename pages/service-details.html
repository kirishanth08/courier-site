Comprehensive Parcel Shipping Solutions

SwiftShip Express offers end-to-end parcel shipping services designed to meet the needs of individuals and businesses alike. Whether you're sending a single package across town or managing high-volume shipments across the country, our logistics network ensures your parcels arrive safely and on time.

Every shipment is backed by our industry-leading insurance coverage of up to $10,000, giving you complete peace of mind. Our fleet of over 2,000 vehicles operates across 50+ metropolitan areas, providing same-day and next-day delivery options to suit your schedule.

Service Features

  • Door-to-door pickup and delivery across 50+ cities nationwide
  • Real-time GPS tracking with SMS and email notifications at every milestone
  • Full insurance coverage up to $10,000 per parcel at no extra cost
  • Flexible pickup scheduling — choose a 2-hour window that works for you
  • Signature confirmation and photo proof of delivery for every shipment
  • Eco-friendly packaging materials and carbon-neutral delivery options
  • Dedicated account manager for business clients with volume discounts

Coverage & Limitations

Our parcel shipping service covers all 50 major metropolitan areas across the United States, with intercity routes connecting over 200 secondary markets. Rural and remote area delivery is available through our extended partner network with a 1–2 day additional transit window.

Weight Limit

Maximum 50 kg per parcel. Packages exceeding this limit should be shipped via our Bulk Cargo service.

Size Restrictions

Max dimensions: 150 cm (L) x 80 cm (W) x 80 cm (H). Combined length + girth must not exceed 300 cm.

Transit Time

Same-day within city. 1–3 business days for intercity. Next-day guaranteed for orders placed before 2 PM.

Packaging

Free branded packaging provided for accounts with 50+ monthly shipments. Standard boxes available at pickup.

Frequently Asked Questions

We accept parcels up to 50 kg in weight with maximum dimensions of 150 cm x 80 cm x 80 cm. The combined length plus girth (2 x width + 2 x height) must not exceed 300 cm. For packages exceeding these limits, please consider our Bulk Cargo service which accommodates oversized freight and palletized shipments.

Every parcel shipped with SwiftShip Express is automatically insured for up to $10,000 at no additional cost. This coverage is included in the base shipping price across all weight tiers. If your parcel's declared value exceeds $10,000, additional coverage can be purchased at 2% of the declared value above the threshold. Claims are processed within 3–5 business days.
