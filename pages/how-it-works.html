The Process

Six Steps to Delivery

Our streamlined process ensures your parcel reaches its destination safely and on time. Here's how it works.

1

Book Online

Schedule a pickup via our website or mobile app in under two minutes. Enter your pickup and delivery details, choose your service level, and confirm your booking. You'll instantly receive a tracking number and estimated delivery window.

2

Pack Your Parcel

Use proper packaging to keep your items safe during transit. Premium account holders receive free branded packaging materials delivered to their door. For standard users, we recommend sturdy boxes with adequate cushioning for fragile items.

3

We Collect

Our professional courier arrives at your doorstep at the scheduled time. No need to drive to a drop-off point — we come to you. You'll receive a real-time notification when the courier is on the way, complete with their name and photo.

4

In Transit

Track your parcel in real-time via GPS on our dashboard. Watch its journey from pickup to delivery with live location updates, estimated arrival times, and milestone notifications at every stage of the delivery route.

5

Out for Delivery

Get notified when your parcel is on its way to the recipient. We send push notifications, SMS, and email alerts so the receiver knows exactly when to expect the delivery. Flexible rescheduling is available if the timing doesn't work.

6

Delivered

Confirmation with photo proof and optional signature. Both sender and receiver get instant delivery confirmation with a timestamped photo of the delivered parcel. An optional recipient signature provides an extra layer of security and proof of delivery.
