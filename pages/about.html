Our Story

Revolutionizing Parcel Delivery Since 2010

SwiftShip Express was founded with a simple mission: to make parcel delivery faster, more reliable, and completely transparent. What started as a small local courier service in Dallas has grown into a nationwide delivery network spanning 50+ cities.

We recognized that traditional courier services were plagued by delayed deliveries, lost packages, and zero visibility. Our founders set out to build a technology-driven delivery company that puts real-time tracking, same-day delivery, and customer satisfaction at the core of everything we do.

Today, we handle over 500,000 deliveries annually with a 98% on-time rate, serving everyone from individual senders to major e-commerce enterprises.
