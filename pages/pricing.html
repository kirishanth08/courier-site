Volumetric Weight

For large but lightweight packages, the shipping rate is based on volumetric (dimensional) weight rather than actual weight to ensure fair pricing.

Calculation Formula

Volumetric Weight = (L × W × H) ÷ 5000

Dimensions in centimeters (cm). Result calculated in kilograms (kg).

The greater of actual weight or volumetric weight is used to calculate the final shipping rate. This ensures consistent and fair pricing across all package sizes.

Volumetric weight is calculated by multiplying the package's length, width, and height (in centimeters) and dividing by 5000. The resulting figure is compared against the actual weight of the package, and the higher value is used to determine the shipping rate.
