How SwiftShip Works

Sending a parcel has never been easier. Four simple steps from your doorstep to delivery.

Step 1

Book Pickup

Schedule a pickup online or via our app. Choose your preferred time slot and delivery speed.

Step 2

We Collect

Our courier arrives at your door, picks up the parcel, and scans it into our tracking system instantly.

Step 3

Track in Transit

Monitor your parcel in real-time via GPS. Get SMS and email notifications at every checkpoint.

Step 4

Delivered

Safe delivery with photo proof and recipient signature. Rate your experience in one tap.
